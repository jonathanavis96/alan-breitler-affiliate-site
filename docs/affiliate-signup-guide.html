What this pack does

This guide walks you through signing up for the four affiliate networks that cover the AI tools currently on AIfocus plus the four new tools launching with the next content drop.

You only need to sign up to the networks, not to each tool individually — once approved on a network, you apply to specific tool programs from inside its dashboard.

  • Total time: ~30 minutes to submit all four applications
  • Approval: 1–7 days, depending on the network
  • Your action items: create accounts, apply to tool programs, send the tracking URLs to me

Why these four networks

The seven AI tools currently on the site, plus the four new tools launching with the next content update, all run their affiliate programmes through one of these four networks. Sign up to all four and you are covered for the full lineup.

Before you start

Gather these once and they will speed every signup up:

  • A bank account or PayPal account — most networks pay by ACH or bank transfer; some support PayPal
  • A US tax form — W-9 if US resident, W-8BEN if not. Networks will prompt you to upload it during signup.
  • The site URL: https://aifocus.work
  • A short site description, e.g. "AIfocus is an independent review site for AI tools used in academic research, writing, and analysis. We score tools across ease of use, academic value, and price-to-value to help researchers, students, and educators pick the right tools."
  • Approximate monthly traffic — even "new site, ramping up" is fine

When asked for the promotional method, pick "content site" or "review site". Some forms ask website vs social — pick website / content.

1. Impact — Grammarly + Otter.ai

1Impact

Covers: Grammarly · Otter.ai
Signup URL https://app.impact.com/secure/sign-up

Steps

  1. Open the signup URL above and click "Sign up as a publisher".
  2. Fill in your basic account details (name, email, password).
  3. On the company-details screen, enter your name or business name, the website https://aifocus.work, and your country.
  4. Verify your email via the link Impact sends.
  5. Once inside the dashboard, go to Brands → Find brands in the left sidebar and search for "Grammarly". Click Apply.
  6. Search for "Otter.ai" and click Apply.

After approval

For each tool, Impact will issue a unique tracking link. Send those to me — I will plug them into the site's affiliate registry within minutes.

Commission  |  Grammarly: $20 per premium signup, $0.20 per free signup, 90-day cookie, $50 minimum payout. Volume bonuses up to $800/mo.
Otter.ai: ~20–25% per sale, 30-day cookie, $10 minimum payout, NET30 (paid 30 days after month-end).

2. PartnerStack — Consensus + QuillBot

2PartnerStack

Covers: Consensus · QuillBot
Signup URL https://market.partnerstack.com/

Steps

  1. Open the signup URL and click "Become a partner" or "Sign up", whichever is visible.
  2. Choose "Marketing partner" or "Affiliate" when prompted.
  3. Fill in your partner profile: name / company name, website (https://aifocus.work), short audience description for academic readers, and tick website / content as promotional methods.
  4. Verify your email.
  5. From the PartnerStack dashboard, click Marketplace in the left sidebar. Search for and apply to Consensus (sometimes filed under "Consensus NLP") and QuillBot.
Commission  |  Consensus: 30% recurring commission for 12 months. Roughly $36 per paying signup at current pricing.
QuillBot: 10–20% recurring (up to ~$20 per subscriber), 30-day cookie. Tier scales with subscriber plan.

3. ShareASale — Scite.ai

3ShareASale

Covers: Scite.ai
Signup URL https://www.shareasale.com/info/affiliates/

Steps

  1. Click "Start" or "Affiliate sign up".
  2. Choose a unique username — this becomes part of your tracking links, so pick something professional (e.g. aifocus-alan).
  3. Fill in personal / business details and verify your email.
  4. ShareASale will ask for your website plus a tax form (W-9 or W-8BEN). Upload both.
  5. Once your account is approved (email confirmation), log in and search the merchant list for "scite". Click Join programme.
Commission  |  Scite.ai: 20% per sale, 30-day cookie (default).

4. Dub.co — Perplexity Pro

4Dub.co

Covers: Perplexity Pro
Signup URL https://partners.dub.co/perplexity/register

Note: use the link above. It's the Perplexity-specific Dub.co referral programme — not the generic Dub.co signup, which is for something different.

Steps

  1. Open the signup URL above.
  2. Enter your email and create a password.
  3. Fill in basic profile info: name, website (https://aifocus.work), country, audience description.
  4. You will get an immediate tracking link or a short approval review — usually approved within minutes.
Commission  |  Perplexity Pro: $10 flat per Pro signup + 10% recurring; $2 bonus per Comet trial. Link-attributed (no traditional cookie — Dub.co tracks by short-link click).

The in-content "go to tool" buttons all use the /go/<slug>/ redirect URL pattern (e.g. /go/grammarly-ai/), so the content side never needs updating — just the redirect destinations.

If a network rejects you

Rare on these four — they're all fairly easy approval bars for a content site. If it does happen:

  • Most rejections cite "insufficient traffic" or "site not ready". Reply to the rejection email with a screenshot of the live site, emphasise it is a new but professionally produced review site, and ask for reconsideration. Approval after appeal is common.
  • If it is a hard "no", let me know and we will look at alternative networks (e.g. CJ Affiliate or Awin sometimes carry overlapping brands).

That's it. Once you have the tracking URLs in hand, send them across in one go. The site has everything else ready and the moment they go in, every "go to tool" button on aifocus.work starts earning revenue.
